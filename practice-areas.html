Comprehensive Legal Counsel

Specialized Practice Areas

Altitude Law Professional Corporation provides authoritative legal representation across nine core disciplines, engineered to protect corporate capital, resolve high-stakes disputes, and enforce cross-border compliance between the United States and Canada.

Corporate tax planning connects entity design, treaty analysis, and capital strategy to the client's cross-border operations.

01 / Fiscal & Corporate Architecture

Corporate Tax Structuring, Wealth Preservation & Cross-Border Capital Growth

Navigating bilateral tax treaties between the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) demands architectural precision. Altitude Law advises multinational corporations, private equity funds, and high-net-worth principals on tax-efficient entity formation, holding company restructuring, and cross-border capital repatriation.

We eliminate double-taxation exposure under the Canada–United States Income Tax Convention while designing resilient asset-protection frameworks. From transfer pricing documentation and outbound investment structuring to succession planning for family enterprises, our counsel safeguards institutional capital at every stage of expansion.

Key Mandates: Cross-border corporate reorganizations, treaty-based withholding tax optimization, controlled foreign corporation (CFC) compliance, and multi-generational wealth trusts.

Complex family matters may involve business interests, property, support, and assets subject to more than one jurisdiction.

02 / Private Client & Domestic Relations

Family Law, High-Net-Worth Divorce & Estate/Asset Division Disputes

When domestic dissolution intersects with complex corporate holdings, international real estate, and multi-jurisdictional trusts, standard family law representation falls short. We provide discreet, strategic advocacy for executives, founders, and prominent families facing high-stakes matrimonial and asset division disputes.

Our practice encompasses the drafting and enforcement of ironclad prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, forensic valuation of closely held businesses, and tracing of offshore or cross-border marital assets. Whether negotiating confidential private settlements or litigating contested custody and spousal support orders, we protect both personal privacy and long-term financial sovereignty.

Key Mandates: High-asset marital dissolution, executive compensation and equity division, international jurisdictional custody disputes, and pre-marital wealth ring-fencing.

Civil and commercial litigation turns on the facts, procedural posture, and applicable law at every stage of a dispute.

03 / Dispute Resolution & Advocacy

Civil & Commercial Litigation, Regulatory Defense & Trial Advocacy

High-stakes commercial conflict requires trial counsel prepared to command the courtroom from the initial pleading through appellate review. Altitude Law represents corporate plaintiffs and defendants in complex breach-of-contract actions, shareholder oppression claims, partnership dissolutions, and bet-the-company tort litigation.

Beyond traditional trial advocacy, we defend enterprises and officers against federal and provincial regulatory investigations, enforcement actions, and parallel cross-border subpoenas. We combine aggressive pre-trial motion practice with disciplined evidentiary strategy to compel favorable settlements or secure decisive verdicts at trial.

Key Mandates: Commercial contract disputes, shareholder derivative litigation, cross-border judgment enforcement, injunctive relief, and regulatory enforcement defense.

Mergers and acquisitions across borders require coordinated diligence, carefully negotiated documents, and jurisdiction-specific analysis.

04 / Transactional & Corporate Law

Cross-Border Contracts, Mergers & Acquisitions & Commercial Transactions

Executing acquisitions, joint ventures, and strategic alliances across the US–Canada border introduces layered statutory, currency, and governance risks. Altitude Law structures, drafts, and negotiates mission-critical commercial agreements engineered to withstand regulatory scrutiny and market volatility.

We guide buyers, sellers, and institutional investors through every phase of the deal lifecycle—from preliminary letters of intent and exhaustive legal due diligence to definitive purchase agreements, indemnification mechanics, and post-closing integration. Every clause is calibrated to allocate risk decisively and protect enterprise value.

Key Mandates: Cross-border M&A transactions, bilateral distribution and supply agreements, strategic joint ventures, private capital raises, and commercial real estate acquisitions.

Public law analysis examines statutory authority, procedural fairness, and the legal standards governing administrative decisions.

05 / Public Law & Judicial Review

Constitutional & Administrative Law, Jurisprudence & Public Law Challenges

When government agencies, licensing tribunals, or statutory authorities exceed their constitutional mandate, decisive public law intervention is required. Altitude Law represents corporations, trade associations, and individuals in judicial review proceedings and constitutional challenges across federal and provincial jurisdictions.

Grounded in rigorous constitutional scholarship, our counsel scrutinizes administrative overreach, procedural fairness violations, and statutory interpretation disputes. We advise clients on regulatory rulemaking, procurement challenges, and charter or constitutional rights protections impacting commercial operations.

Key Mandates: Judicial review of administrative tribunal decisions, constitutional and statutory validity challenges, professional licensing defense, and public procurement disputes.

Intellectual property and compliance programs help organizations address ownership, licensing, privacy, and governance obligations.

06 / Intangible Assets & Governance

Intellectual Property, Data Governance & Corporate Compliance

In modern enterprise, proprietary technology, trade secrets, and brand equity represent core balance-sheet assets. Altitude Law establishes comprehensive intellectual property protection and cross-border licensing architectures to defend proprietary innovations across North American markets.

Concurrently, we design enterprise-wide corporate compliance and cybersecurity governance programs aligned with evolving US federal/state privacy statutes and Canadian PIPEDA/provincial data protection regimes. We conduct internal compliance audits, draft technology transfer agreements, and enforce trade secret non-disclosure covenants.

Key Mandates: Cross-border IP licensing and technology transfers, trade secret protection and enforcement, data privacy compliance (US/Canada), and corporate board governance frameworks.

Estate and fiduciary disputes concern the administration of assets, the duties owed to beneficiaries, and available equitable remedies.

07 / Trusts, Estates & Equity

Fiduciary Duty, Estate Litigation & Equitable Remedies

Fiduciaries, trustees, executors, and corporate directors are bound by the highest standards of loyalty and prudence under equity. When those duties are breached through self-dealing, mismanagement, or improper asset diversion, Altitude Law prosecutes and defends complex fiduciary and estate litigation.

We represent beneficiaries, institutional trustees, and estate executors in contested will challenges, trust accounting disputes, capacity and undue influence proceedings, and cross-border probate administration. Where immediate asset preservation is critical, we seek equitable remedies including constructive trusts, tracing orders, and freezing injunctions.

Key Mandates: Breach of fiduciary duty litigation, contested estate and trust administration, cross-border probate coordination, and equitable tracing and constructive trust remedies.

Thoughtful succession planning coordinates family objectives, asset ownership, and the legal requirements of each relevant jurisdiction.

08 / Private Wealth & Succession

Inheritance & Wealth Succession

The orderly transfer of family and business wealth calls for careful planning across generations and, where relevant, national borders. Altitude Law advises individuals, families, and business owners on succession structures designed to reflect their objectives and the legal frameworks that govern their assets.

Our counsel addresses estate and trust planning, business succession, and coordination of ownership and governance documents. For families with property or beneficiaries in more than one jurisdiction, we help identify cross-border considerations and coordinate appropriate local advice.

Key Mandates: Intergenerational wealth-transfer planning, business succession, estate and trust structures, coordination of cross-border estate documents, and executor and trustee guidance.

Investment activity across markets depends on sound transaction documents and careful attention to applicable securities and regulatory requirements.

09 / International Finance & Securities

Cross-Border Investments & Capital Markets

Raising capital or investing across the United States and Canada can engage distinct securities, disclosure, corporate, and foreign-investment rules. Altitude Law advises businesses, investors, and private capital participants on structuring and documenting cross-border transactions with attention to the requirements of each relevant market.

We assist with transaction diligence, investment and subscription documentation, governance arrangements, and coordination of regulatory analysis with relevant local counsel. Our approach is tailored to the transaction, investor profile, and jurisdictions involved; applicable registration, exemption, and disclosure requirements are assessed on a matter-specific basis.

Key Mandates: Cross-border private investments, capital-raising documentation, securities and regulatory diligence, investor rights and governance, and coordination of US–Canada transaction counsel.
